PULSE MONITOR V2.0

A clearer view of your data

This dashboard displays heart-rate and SpO₂ estimates uploaded to ThingSpeak by the project’s optical sensor. It does not measure ECG, body temperature, or respiratory rate.

The live review uses configurable thresholds. The experimental Isolation Forest service runs in the full local system and is not connected to this public view.

Sensor estimates and review flags are not medical diagnoses. Do not use this prototype for emergency decisions or to change treatment.

Journal entries and dashboard preferences remain in this browser. The public feed is read-only.
